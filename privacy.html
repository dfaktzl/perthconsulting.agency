Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 — Perth Consulting & The Collective Investigation Agency ("we", "our", or "us") are committed to protecting the privacy, safety, and strict confidentiality of every individual who contacts our agency.

1. Compliance with Australian Privacy Principles (APP)

Our data handling practices strictly adhere to the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles. Because of the sensitive nature of relationship investigations and domestic vulnerability support, our confidentiality standards exceed standard commercial requirements.

2. Information Collection & Safe Handling

We collect only information strictly necessary to evaluate your inquiry and conduct authorized Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) searches:

  • Contact details (Aliases, safe unmonitored email addresses, Signal/Telegram IDs).
  • Case briefing notes and target parameters (usernames, phone numbers, public social media handles).
  • Communication logs strictly encrypted in our secure vault.

3. Zero-Knowledge Evidence Delivery & Automatic 30-Day Shredding

Once an evidentiary report is delivered to your designated safe contact channel, all raw case materials, target searches, and investigative files are permanently purged and cryptographically shredded from our servers after 30 days unless legal preservation is explicitly requested in writing by the client.

4. Stealth Billing & Financial Anonymity

To ensure your partner cannot trace your inquiry through joint credit card or bank statements, all transactions are billed discreetly under neutral merchant descriptions with PayPal Buyer Protection.

5. Domestic Violence Safety & Non-Disclosure

We never disclose client identities, case existence, or inquiries to third parties under any commercial circumstances. All staff and specialist contractors operate under legally binding Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).
