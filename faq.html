Q. Will my partner ever know they are being investigated?

No. Our methodology is 100% passive and covert. We utilize external open-source intelligence (OSINT), public databases, reverse digital footprints, and server-side metadata audits. We never send messages, make contact, or perform actions that would alert the target.

Q. How does payment appear on my bank statement?

We enforce strict stealth billing protocols. Transactions appear under a neutral corporate advisory name without any mention of infidelity, cheating, or private investigations. All payments are processed through secure PayPal gateways with Buyer Protection.

Q. What proof will I receive at the end?

You receive an encrypted PDF and multimedia evidentiary brief containing timestamped screenshots, verified dating profiles, social media alt-accounts, geolocation data, and an analytical summary written by our senior investigators.

Q. What if the investigation proves my partner is NOT cheating?

A clean bill of health is one of the best outcomes. In about 20% of cases, suspicious behaviors are caused by surprise financial stress, undisclosed health challenges, or toxic third-party interference. We provide a full verification audit so you can clear the air with certainty.

Q. How do I safely access my report?

We only send final reports to the safe email or Signal/Telegram account you provide during intake. We recommend using private/incognito browsing and a device your partner cannot access.
